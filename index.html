✎ Who I am

I'm Mahnaz Gharehbaghi — friends call me Mahi. I'm a UI/UX designer who cares about making everyday tasks feel quick and effortless.

My process starts with understanding the people I'm designing for, then mapping their journey, sketching wireframes and building a consistent visual system that makes the final product easy to use and easy to build.
